These projects show my work across research methods, team leadership and collaborative applications. Project roles below refer to my work within the named organisations and teams.

## Royal Military Academy

### [GENIUS](https://mecatron.rma.ac.be/index.php/projects/genius/) · European Defence Fund

I lead RMA's research team in this project on robotic detection and disposal of explosive threats. My work includes robotic manipulation, shared autonomy and coordination with consortium partners.

### [ATTENTION](https://researchportal.rma.ac.be/en/projects/advanced-target-detection-recognition-and-identification-based-on/) · Belgian Defence / DEFRA

I serve as promotor alongside colleagues on research into sensing and navigation for quadruped robots used in explosive ordnance disposal.

### [eUGS](https://researchportal.rma.ac.be/en/projects/collaborative-combat-engineering-unmanned-ground-systems/) · European Defence Agency

I serve as promotor alongside colleagues on collaborative unmanned ground systems, including manipulation and teleoperation for hazardous tasks.

### [STEALTH](https://researchportal.rma.ac.be/en/projects/swarm-technology-for-enhanced-autonomous-flight-and-tactical-hand/) · European Defence Fund

I am listed as promotor with colleagues on this project concerning autonomous navigation and coordination of aerial systems.

## KU Leuven

### [ROBOTGENSKILL](https://robotgenskill.pages.gitlab.kuleuven.be/) · ERC Advanced Grant

As a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Joris De Schutter's group, I developed trajectory representations for learning and generalising skills from human demonstrations. I also helped coordinate the research team and co-supervised doctoral researchers.

### [PROROB](https://researchportal.vub.be/en/projects/icon-project-prorob-vrar-based-robot-trajectory-programming) · Flanders Make ICON

I led the KU Leuven team working on virtual-reality approaches to programming robot tasks and their translation to industrial applications.

## Profile

I am a mechanical engineer and researcher with experience in robot learning, motion planning and autonomous systems. My work has grown from developing research methods to leading project teams and supervising researchers. I work across academic and applied research, from industrial robot programming to autonomous systems for hazardous environments.

## Experience

### Senior Researcher, Royal Military Academy, Brussels — 2025–present

Robotics & Autonomous Systems unit, Department of Mechanics. I lead RMA's team in the European Defence Fund [GENIUS](https://mecatron.rma.ac.be/index.php/projects/genius/) project and serve as a promotor with colleagues on [ATTENTION](https://researchportal.rma.ac.be/en/projects/advanced-target-detection-recognition-and-identification-based-on/) and [eUGS](https://researchportal.rma.ac.be/en/projects/collaborative-combat-engineering-unmanned-ground-systems/). My responsibilities include guiding researchers, coordinating project work and collaborating with partner organisations.

### Postdoctoral Researcher, KU Leuven — 2018–2024

Robotics Research Group, Department of Mechanical Engineering. I worked on the ERC Advanced Grant ROBOTGENSKILL led by Prof. Joris De Schutter, advancing representations of human-demonstrated skills for robot learning and control. I led KU Leuven's research team in the Flanders Make PROROB project, co-supervised two PhD researchers and mentored master's thesis students. I also coordinated teaching-assistant duties in the Automation division.

### Doctoral Researcher, KU Leuven — 2013–2018

I developed invariant representations of rigid-body motion trajectories for motion recognition and robot learning from demonstration. My doctoral research was supervised by Prof. Joris De Schutter. I also assisted with engineering courses and student projects.

## Education

- **PhD in Mechanical Engineering**, KU Leuven, 2018.
- **MSc in Mechanical Engineering**, KU Leuven, 2013.

## About

I am a Senior Researcher in the [Robotics & Autonomous Systems unit](https://mecatron.rma.ac.be/index.php/people/) at the [Royal Military Academy](https://www.rma.ac.be/en) (RMA) in Brussels. I lead and mentor researchers in collaborative projects while continuing to work on robotics and autonomous systems research. I am particularly interested in how robots can learn useful skills, work with people and operate reliably in changing environments.

## Research and projects

At RMA, I lead the Academy's research team in [GENIUS](https://mecatron.rma.ac.be/index.php/projects/genius/), a European Defence Fund project on robotic demining. I also serve as a promotor alongside colleagues on [ATTENTION](https://researchportal.rma.ac.be/en/projects/advanced-target-detection-recognition-and-identification-based-on/) and [eUGS](https://researchportal.rma.ac.be/en/projects/collaborative-combat-engineering-unmanned-ground-systems/). These projects involve sensing, navigation, manipulation and human oversight of autonomous systems. My work includes guiding researchers, shaping project tasks and working with research partners.

Before joining RMA in 2025, I spent six years as a postdoctoral researcher at KU Leuven. Within the ERC-funded [ROBOTGENSKILL](https://robotgenskill.pages.gitlab.kuleuven.be/) project, I developed methods for representing and generalizing human-demonstrated robot skills. I also led the KU Leuven team in [PROROB](https://researchportal.vub.be/en/projects/icon-project-prorob-vrar-based-robot-trajectory-programming), a Flanders Make project on programming robots in virtual reality.

My research interests include learning from demonstration, robot manipulation, shared autonomy, motion planning, sensor-based control and numerical optimization. See my [projects](/projects/) and [publications](/publications/) for examples across industrial and field robotics.

## Supervision and teaching

At KU Leuven, I was co-promotor of two PhD researchers and mentored more than 15 master's thesis students. I have also supported engineering teaching and coordinated teaching-assistant duties. At RMA, supervision and development of early-career researchers are a central part of my role. More detail is available on my [supervision and teaching page](/teaching/).

## Research software

I develop the open-source [invariants_mat](https://github.com/maximvochten/invariants_mat) and [invariants_py](https://github.com/maximvochten/invariants_py) packages for trajectory invariant methods. The [Trajectory Invariants](https://trajectory-invariants.github.io/) site provides documentation and examples.

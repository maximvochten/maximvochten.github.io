## Research supervision

Supporting researchers as they develop their ideas and publish results is a central part of my work. At the Royal Military Academy, I supervise researchers across robotics and autonomous systems projects and support their scientific development.

At KU Leuven, I was co-promotor of two doctoral researchers working on representations of demonstrated robot skills:

- [Arno Verduyn](https://www.kuleuven.be/wieiswie/en/person/0140902) (2020–2025): analysis, recognition and generalization of human-demonstrated skills.
- [Riccardo Burlizzi](https://www.kuleuven.be/wieiswie/en/person/0148800) (2021–2025): data augmentation in robot learning from demonstration.

I also mentored and co-supervised more than 15 master's thesis students in mechanical engineering and artificial intelligence at KU Leuven.

## Teaching and student activities

I was a teaching assistant for **Applied Mechanics 3** and **Mechanisms and Vibration** during my doctoral research. As a postdoctoral researcher, I coordinated the allocation of teaching-assistant duties within the Automation division of KU Leuven's Department of Mechanical Engineering.

Outside regular courses, I organised and presented hands-on robot-building workshops and the annual student Sumo Robot Competition through the IEEE Student Branch Leuven.
